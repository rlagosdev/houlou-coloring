Questions parents ask

What exactly do I get?

A printable PDF coloring book starring Houlou the baby owl and his friends. You download it once and print the pages whenever you like.

What age is it for?

It's made for children aged 3 to 7, with thick, simple outlines. Younger and older children enjoy it too.

How do I pay?

By card, through Stripe's secure checkout. It's a one-time payment — €4.90 for the book or €11.99 for the pack of 4 printable ebooks — no subscription.

What is a mystery picture?

A color-by-number page: each number matches a crayon, and a hidden picture of Houlou appears as your child colors. The answers are at the end of the book.

How does the shop work?

T-shirts, bodysuits, posters, tote bags and mugs are printed on demand for you by our print partner and shipped in about 5–10 business days. Shipping is free and included in the price. You receive a tracking number by email.

How will I get it?

Instantly: the download link appears right after payment, and you also get a receipt by email.
